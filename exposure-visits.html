Exposure Visits for Scholars:

Exposure visits are designed to promote peer learning, inter-institutional bonding, and academic exchange among scholars. These visits encourage cross-learning, build confidence and communication skills, and foster collaboration across institutions. Activities often include student and faculty interactions, research or project showcases, and sessions with clubs or alumni. The expected outcomes are a stronger scholar community, fresh perspectives, and documented learnings for future batches. Next steps include identifying host colleges, scheduling visits with necessary logistics, and collecting participant feedback.
