Exposure visits

Exposure Visits for Scholars:

Exposure visits are designed to promote peer learning, inter-institutional bonding, and academic exchange among scholars. These visits aim to encourage cross-learning, build confidence and communication, and foster collaboration across institutions. Activities typically include student and faculty interactions, research/project showcases, and sessions with clubs or alumni. The expected outcomes are a stronger scholar community, new perspectives, and documented learnings for future batches. As next steps, host colleges will be identified, visits scheduled with necessary logistics, and feedback collected from participants.
