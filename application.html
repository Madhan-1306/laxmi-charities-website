Application Process

1. Institute Selection

- Premium institutes will be identified and contacted by Laxmi Charities.

2. Student Nomination

- Each institution will shortlist eligible students based on the criteria provided by Laxmi Charities.

3. Verification

- The institution will submit the final list of selected students along with an attestation confirming eligibility (annual parental income and academic performance as required).

4. Application Submission

- A scholarship application link will then be shared with the shortlisted students.

- Students must complete and submit the online application form to Laxmi Charities

5. Scholarship Disbursement

- Upon approval, the scholarship amount will be disbursed directly to the students.
